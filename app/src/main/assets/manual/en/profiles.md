# Profiles and templates

Settings > Profiles and templates lets you change many settings in one step. **Templates** are ready-made sets that come with the app. **Profiles** are your own saved sets of settings. You can also reach this screen from the Everyday section at the top of the settings.

## Templates

Four templates are built into the app. They are public and the same on every phone; they hold no data of yours.

- **Minimal**: no number row, hints or key sound. Haptic feedback stays on.
- **Bulgarian daily**: English (QWERTY) and Bulgarian (phonetic layout), the language key, smart quotes and glide typing on. You can change the Bulgarian layout afterwards under [Languages and layouts](page:languages).
- **Maximum privacy**: incognito always on, no clipboard history, no learning, no voice key, no next-word model, covered-window blocking. It only makes privacy stricter, never looser.
- **Large, one-handed**: a taller keyboard, larger text and one-handed mode on in portrait.

## Your profiles

- **Save the current settings as a profile**: give it a name of 1 to 40 characters. At most 10 profiles can be saved, and two cannot have the same name.
- **Apply** a profile or a template: Taen first shows the list of the settings that would change ("N settings will change"). Confirm to apply them in one step. If nothing would change, it says so.
- **Rename** and **Delete** a profile. **Delete all profiles** removes every saved profile; your current settings stay as they are.

A profile can hold the appearance, layouts, typing, toolbar, gesture, feedback and language-switch settings. It never holds your learned words, personal dictionary, snippets, clipboard, the incognito app list, per-app languages, free text, files, the self-destruct settings, the app-lock PIN, or other profiles.

## The privacy rule

Privacy settings (incognito, the secure window, the accessibility warning, the windows-over-the-keyboard mode, clipboard history and its limits, personalized learning, adding to the personal dictionary, remembering language per app, the voice key) are stored in a profile, but **applying a profile or template can only make them stricter, never looser**. The apply screen says "Privacy settings are only made stricter, never looser." To loosen a privacy setting, change it yourself under Settings > Privacy and Preferences.

## Where profiles are kept

Profiles are stored encrypted. They are empty and read-only while the device is locked, are included in the encrypted backup, and are deleted by a self-destruct whose scope includes settings. If the saved profiles are damaged, they are shown as damaged: save and apply are refused, and only **Delete all profiles** removes them. See [Privacy explained](page:privacy).
