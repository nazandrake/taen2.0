# GIFs and stickers

Settings > GIFs and stickers holds your own pictures and GIFs, stored encrypted on the phone, and a small starter set that comes with the app. You send them from the keyboard into apps that accept images. Taen has no online GIF search: nothing is fetched from the internet.

## Use them

1. Import pictures here (below).
2. Turn on the **GIFs and stickers** key in the toolbar ([Toolbar](page:toolbar)), then tap it in a chat to open the picture panel.
3. Tap a picture to send it. It works only in fields that announce that they accept that kind of picture. In other fields the panel says "This field does not accept images".

If the panel says it is locked, unlock the phone. If it says the list is damaged, open this screen: only **Delete all pictures** is available then.

## Settings

### {#media_starter_pack}
Shows the starter set in the picture panel: a small set of emoji stickers (Microsoft Fluent Emoji, MIT licence) and 36 animated GIFs (Noto Animated Emoji by Google, CC BY 4.0, scaled to 128 pixels). They are part of the app and are never stored or exported. Default: off. Turn it on if you want something to send before you import your own.

## Your pictures

- **Import pictures** opens the file picker. GIF, PNG, JPEG and WebP files are accepted, up to 8 MiB each, up to 500 pictures and 256 MiB in total. Location and other metadata is removed from the copy that Taen keeps; the picture itself is not re-encoded. A file that is not accepted shows the reason: too big, limit reached, not a picture, damaged, phone locked.
- Each picture has up to 8 **tags**. Tap **Tags** to edit them. The **Search tags** field and the panel use them to find a picture.
- **Delete** removes one picture. **Delete all pictures** removes every imported picture and the list. The starter set stays in the app.
- A line shows how many pictures you have and how much of the limit they use.

## Privacy

Pictures are stored as encrypted files. They are included in the encrypted backup when the whole export is at most 64 MiB, and are deleted by a self-destruct that includes imported data. A picture is handed to the app through one temporary link that works for ten minutes, is not shared with anything else, and is revoked at the lock, at a wipe and when the field changes. See [Privacy explained](page:privacy).
